---------------------
       Kitchen
    -------------
On the dining table lay a watermelon that had been cut in half; one half had already been eaten, while the other had begun to give off a foul odor. A trash can filled with fruit peels was placed next to the dining table.

He carefully searched the kitchen. If his hunch was correct, this was the true crime scene.

The kitchen had been cleaned; there were no obvious traces of blood.

He sat down to examine the drainpipe and discovered a few stray hairs caught along its rim-short, black strands roughly three centimeters in length, belonging to a male. On the wall behind the pipe, several drops of blood remained, having escaped the cleanup.

Aside from this, no further discoveries were made in either the refrigerator or the kitchen cabinets.

On the dining table, situated opposite the kitchen, stood a massive bouquet of roses. Arranged in an exquisite glass vase, the water within remained crystal clear, and the roses themselves were in magnificent, full bloom. A portion of the flowers appeared to have been cut away - suggesting that the petals scattered throughout the bedroom had likely been plucked from this very bouquet.
