      Main Bedroom
      ------------

The moment he stepped into the room, Tieu Lau caught the scent of a strange stench, mingling with the fragrance of roses already lingering in the air.

A double bed stood in the center of the master bedroom, currently blanketed in fresh red roses; atop the petals lay a woman clad in a white silk nightgown. Her hands rested gently upon her abdomen, and at a glance, she appeared to be sleeping peacefully.

Yet, blood was gushing from her chest - a blindingly vivid crimson.

The bodice of her nightgown was stained scarlet with blood, and a pool of the crimson fluid had begun to form upon her exquisitely made-up face. Her lips were cyanotic, her skin as pale and lifeless as paper; the pungent, foul odor permeating the air was, in fact, the stench of this woman's corpse.

Tieu Lau steeled himself against the stench of the corpse and stepped forward to begin a thorough examination of the victim. The victim was an adult female, approximately thirty years old, dressed in sleepwear, and had died inside her home.

Her chest was heavily stained with blood; the fatal injury was a single stab wound to the heart. The murder weapon was evidently a sharp blade, driven directly into the aorta - a swift and decisive strike. Aside from this, there were no other injuries on the victim's body, nor were there any signs of abuse.

Tieu Lau glanced at the magnificent bed, strewn with a lavish display of flowers, and quickly reached a conclusion: this was definitely not the primary crime scene!

Tieu Lau's area of ​​expertise was forensic medicine - specifically, toxicology. However, having been an avid reader of detective novels since childhood and a passionate enthusiast of escape rooms, he also possessed a keen understanding of the principles of deductive reasoning.

When police investigate a homicide, locating the actual crime scene serves as an invaluable aid in solving the case.

The reason Tieu Lau was able to determine that this was not the primary crime scene was, in truth, quite simple.

The heart acts as the primary blood supply center for the entire human body; much like a powerful pump, with every beat, it propels blood to every extremity. The victim’s fatal wound was located in the heart itself—a knife thrust driven directly into the aorta. Had the victim been stabbed to death upon this very bed, the torrent of blood unleashed by the severed aorta would have splattered across the pillows and sheets, and likely even sprayed onto the surrounding walls.

Yet, the surroundings remained pristine - utterly devoid of any bloodstains.

The victim's nightgown was soaked with blood across the chest area; however, the total volume of blood loss was estimated at a mere 100cc - less than half the amount typically drawn during a standard blood donation. It was patently obvious that the victim had been murdered elsewhere, and only after nearly all the blood had drained away did the killer transport the body to the bed in the bedroom.

To commit murder, drain the victim's blood, and then lay the corpse upon a bed blanketed in flower petals... the killer was such a psychopath.

A wardrobe is placed against the wall. In the left corner of the room stands a dressing table.

This is the master bedroom, and it also has a private bathroom.
