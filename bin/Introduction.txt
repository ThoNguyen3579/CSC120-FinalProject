Introduction
_______________________
Just as he was crossing the street, a bus rounding the corner suddenly lost control and hurtled rapidly toward Tieu Lau. His eyes went wide, and his heart nearly stopped beating!
He scrambled to get away, but the bus was moving too fast; the instant he moved, his entire body was flung into the air.
Everything went black before his eyes, and Tieu Lau quickly lost consciousness.
When he came to, he found himself facing a vast expanse of pure white mist.
From within the hazy fog, a cold, mechanical voice rang out:
[Congratulations, Tieu Lau. You have been selected by the Card World. You may now choose one of the following two options:
A. Rest in peace and forget everything. From this moment on, your soul will be completely extinguished, forever released from the cycle of birth, aging, sickness, and death.
B. Be reborn. Retain your memories and enter the Card World - but you must accept its challenges. However, the rules of survival within the Card World are incredibly brutal; indeed, merely existing there may prove to be a fate far worse than simply choosing death.]
Tieu Lau was somewhat bewildered. Was this voice merely a hallucination? And offering him two such bizarre choices, at that?
Seeing that he offered no response, the cold, mechanical voice spoke up again: [You have 10 seconds to decide. Once 10 seconds have elapsed, memory erasure will commence. 10... 9... 8...]
Tieu Lau answered decisively: "I choose B."
Regardless of whether this voice was a hallucination or not, between these two options, he could only choose the second. He was only 28 years old; he hadn't lived nearly long enough. He had no desire to have his memories wiped and vanish into oblivion. All he needed was a chance. No matter how difficult or dangerous this new world might be, at least he would still be alive - wouldn't he?
As long as there is life, there is hope. Tieu Lau quickly regained his composure and asked, "Who are you?"
The other party replied coldly: [I am the System Spirit of the Card World - a highly intelligent entity. You cannot see me, but I can see you.]
The sensation of being under surveillance made Tieu Lau frown involuntarily; he took a deep breath and continued to ask, "What is this 'Card World' you speak of?"
Immediately thereafter, countless cards suddenly materialized before his eyes.
They were the very playing cards he had used to play with his younger siblings on New Year's Eve - their backs bearing the familiar blue diamond pattern, the most common variety typically found on the shelves of any corner store. The 5 of Hearts, 8 of Diamonds, 10 of Clubs, Jack of Spades... Cards displaying a myriad of different suits and values ​​formed a long, continuous stream, flying right up to his face before whizzing past him as if he were watching a film sped up fourfold.
In the white mist, the cards quickly arranged themselves horizontally and vertically, the four suits mixing together irregularly, the densely packed poker cards forming a giant wall in 10 seconds.
[Welcome, player Tieu Lau, to the Card World.]
Standing before the wall of cards, Tieu Lau was dazzled by their sheer number. He noticed that, except for the number and suit in the upper left corner of the card, like the 2 of Hearts and the 3 of Spades, the surface of each card displayed an animated image. These cards were like countless frozen spaces, each card like a small TV, playing different scenes.
The card right in front of him was displaying the clearest image.
Tieu Lau looked closer and saw that in that small world, a short girl was being chased by a group of ugly zombies. The girl accidentally slipped and fell to the ground, and the zombies immediately surrounded her, tearing off her head and chewing it noisily. The girl's terrified gaze pierced through the card, staring straight at Tieu Lau.
Tieu Lau's scalp tingled, and his stomach churned with nausea. His hands clenched tightly; the intense pain of his fingernails digging into his palms told him this wasn't a dream, but a real, otherworldly space constructed from cards that he couldn't explain.
That girl certainly couldn't survive, but this "brain-eating zombie" method of death was truly too cruel…
Was she also inexplicably dragged into the Card World like him?
Tieu Lau closed his eyes, unable to bear watching any longer.
The system coldly asked: [Have you seen the Card World?]
Tieu Lau took a deep breath and said, "Yes."
System: [You only have one chance to choose again, A or B, which one do you choose?]
Tieu Lau didn't hesitate at all: "I choose B."
System: [Confirm selection.]
[Congratulations, you are the 86,587,847th challenger. Your initial data for the challenger Tieu Lau has been successfully saved.]
[Next, please read the rules of the new world.]
[ In the Card World, each card is a different small world, which players can call a secret chamber.
The type of secret chamber is determined by its color and pattern.
Hearts Secret Chamber: Detective case solving; Diamonds Secret Chamber: Maze, decoding; Spades Secret Chamber: Survival elimination; Clubs Secret Chamber: PvP challenge. Only by completing the Secret Chamber challenge can you return to the human world and obtain a new, true life.
Successful challenges earn rewards. Failed challenges will send you to the Nightmare Secret Chamber for punishment. Players who cannot pass the Nightmare Secret Chamber will be completely eliminated by the Card World.] The rules are only stated once; please proceed to the labyrinth. The first labyrinth will contain the secret chambers [2 of Hearts] and [2 of Diamonds].
[Challenger data for Tieu Lau has been read.]
Tieu Lau 's vision suddenly went dark.
When his vision returned, he found himself in a long, narrow, empty corridor with several turns. The light in the corridor was dim.
He has entered the maze.
