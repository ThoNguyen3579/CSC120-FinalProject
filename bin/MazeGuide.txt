The maze consists of 49 cells arranged in a 7 x 7 grid, as shown above. Each cell is numbered in order: row 1 contains cells 0 to 6, row 2 contains cells 7 to 13, and this pattern continues up to row 7, which contains cells 42 to 48.
You begin in cell 1. To move through the maze, use the commands go up, go down, go left, and go right. If you attempt to move into a wall, the system will let you know.
As you explore, it is helpful to sketch your own map so you can keep track of where you’ve been and avoid getting lost.
Most cells are simply pathways, but some cells contain puzzle rooms. These special rooms include clues, evidence, or riddles. When you reach one, you will see a door indicating that a puzzle is inside. You can choose to open the door and enter, or continue exploring and return later.
There are two exits hidden in the maze: the Hearts exit and the Diamonds exit, and you have to answer a question related to the secret chambers to get out through the exit.
