-----------------------
        Bathroom
        --------
He entered the bathroom. Scattered across the floor were numerous long strands of hair - curly brown hair that perfectly matched the victim's characteristics. They had likely fallen out while the victim was bathing - while still alive - and had not yet been cleared away. Additionally, there was a change of clothes in the bathroom, bearing a few dark crimson stains.

The bathtub and toilet were spotless, and the toiletries arranged on the sink showed no signs of anything unusual. There was some used toilet paper in the trash can, which looked dirty.
