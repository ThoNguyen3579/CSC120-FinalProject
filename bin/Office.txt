          Office
      --------------

A bookcase filled an entire wall; it was stocked exclusively with medical texts, including several specialized volumes on cardiac surgery. Beside the bookcase stood a standard desk, topped with a single desk lamp. To the right of the desk was a stack of three drawers, while to the left sat a small cabinet fitted with a hinged door.
Tieu Lau opened the drawers and discovered that the top compartment contained a passport and a medical practice license - both belonging to the male owner of the house. At last, he had obtained information regarding the husband: Trieu Sam, born October 26, 1988, of Han ethnicity, and a general surgeon.
In the other drawers, he found various property bills, bank receipts, and medical research papers-items that offered no assistance to the case.
He closed the drawers and opened the small cabinet door located in the bottom-left corner.
Inside the cabinet sat a black safe. It featured a four-digit passcode system with a keypad arranged in standard vertical order: 123, 456, 789, and *0#. He had encountered this type of safe many times before; one simply needed to enter the correct passcode and press the # key to unlock it.
