**Commands**
- go up, go down, go left, go right: move between cells in the maze.
- search [Object]: check every interactive things.
- code [Put code here]: if you want to answer the safe in the office, put it that way (only work when you are in the office)
- inventory: show evidence collected
- help: show available commands
- enter bathroom: enter the private bathroom of the main bedroom
- look: look around this secret chamber's room (applied only to heart chamber)

**Layout Description**
The game is a map with two exits.
- You start at cell 0.
- You can move through the map to explore different cells.
- The Diamond exit is in cell 27 and the Heart exit is in cell 48. 

**Game Modes**
- Easy(ier) mode: look at the map right at the start of the game and go through that map.
- Hard mode: do not look at the map, just use a pen and paper to figure out the map yourself. If you hit the wall the system will inform you.

**Challenges**
- Some cells contain clues or riddles that you need to solve while exploring the map.
- When you reach either the Diamond exit or the Heart exit, a final question related to the chosen room will appear.
- You win the game by answering the final question correctly.
